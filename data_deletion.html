Account & data deletion

There are three things you might want to remove: one player, everything on a device, or the whole account. The first two the app does by itself, at once.

Delete one player — in the app

Open that player in SYVT and choose Delete this player, then confirm. Their nickname, avatar, scores and settings leave the device immediately, and if you are signed in they are removed from your account and from every other device signed in to it.

If you have never signed in, this is the only deletion there is to make: without an account, nothing about your players ever left the device, and we hold nothing to delete.

Delete everything on a device

Uninstalling SYVT removes the device's copy of everything — all players, scores and settings. If you were signed in, the account's copy stays where it is, so use the request below to remove that as well.

Delete your account and all of its data

  1. Write to hi@SYVT.me from the email address the account uses.
  2. Use the subject line DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. By default, every account held under your email address for apps published by A51 will be deleted — mention SYVT in the message if you want only this game's data removed.

Deleting your account does not cancel a SYVT Pro subscription, and does not undo a purchase: Google Play handles billing separately. Cancel a subscription first under Google Play subscriptions.

What happens next

  • Deleting a player in the app takes effect immediately. Emailed requests are carried out within 5 business days.
  • We remove the sign-in record (the email address and the stored password hash), the account's document with every player under it, and any record of a friends-and-family code the account redeemed.
  • Deletion is permanent: nicknames, avatars, scores and settings cannot be recovered.
  • Residual copies in operational backups are purged within up to 365 days.

Need help?

Contact hi@SYVT.me. What we store in the first place is described in the Privacy Policy.
