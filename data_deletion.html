Account & data deletion

The app does all of this itself, and does it at once: the whole account, every player's scores, one player, or the lot. There is an email route at the end for anyone who cannot open the app.

Delete your account — in the app

Open SYVT and tap Settings (for parents) at the foot of the start screen. At the bottom of that page, hold Danger zone for a second to open it, choose Delete account, and confirm with your password. The sign-in and everything backed up under it leave our servers immediately.

The players themselves stay on the device, so nobody loses their name or their face; they are simply no longer backed up. To remove them from the device as well, delete each player as below, or uninstall the app.

Deleting your account does not cancel a SYVT+ subscription, and does not undo a purchase: Google Play handles billing separately. Cancel a subscription first under Google Play subscriptions.

Delete all statistics

In the same danger zone, Delete all statistics wipes the games played, the points and the best scores of every player on the device, and in the account if one is signed in. Names, avatars, worlds and settings stay as they are.

Delete one player

Open that player in SYVT and choose Delete this player, then confirm. Their nickname, avatar, scores and settings leave the device immediately, and if you are signed in they are removed from your account and from every other device signed in to it.

If you have never signed in, this is the only deletion there is to make: without an account, nothing about your players ever left the device, and we hold nothing to delete.

Delete everything on a device

Uninstalling SYVT removes the device's copy of everything — all players, scores and settings.

If you cannot open the app: by email

  1. Write to hi@SYVT.me from the email address the account uses.
  2. Use the subject line DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. By default, every account held under your email address for apps published by A51 will be deleted — mention SYVT in the message if you want only this game's data removed.

What happens next

  • Everything done in the app takes effect immediately. Emailed requests are carried out within 5 business days.
  • Deleting the account removes the sign-in record (the email address and the stored password hash), the account's document with every player under it, and any record of a friends-and-family code it redeemed.
  • Deletion is permanent: nicknames, avatars, scores and settings cannot be recovered.
  • Residual copies in operational backups are purged within up to 365 days.

Need help?

Contact hi@SYVT.me. What we store in the first place is described in the Privacy Policy.
