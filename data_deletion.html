Account & data deletion

The app does all of this itself, and does it at once, on Android and on iPhone and iPad alike: the whole account, every player's scores, one player, or the lot. There is an email route at the end for anyone who cannot open the app.

Delete your account — in the app

Open SYVT and tap Settings (for parents) at the foot of the start screen. At the bottom of that page, hold Danger zone for a second to open it, choose Delete account, and confirm it: with your password, with Google if the account signs in with Google, or, on an iPhone or iPad, with Apple if it signs in with Apple. The sign-in and all of the account's data on our servers are deleted immediately.

The players themselves stay on the device, so nobody loses their name or their face; they are simply no longer backed up. If the device has players of its own besides the account's and there is no room for all of them, the app asks which ones stay before it deletes anything. To remove them from the device as well, delete each player as below, or uninstall the app.

With Sign in with Apple, confirming with Apple also lets the app withdraw SYVT from your Apple Account. Removing SYVT under Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple on the iPhone stops it on Apple's side only: the SYVT account and its data stay on our servers until the account is deleted, in the app or by email.

Deleting your account does not cancel a SYVT+ subscription, and does not undo a purchase: the store handles billing separately. Cancel a subscription first: on Android under Google Play subscriptions; on an iPhone or iPad in Settings, under your name → Subscriptions, or on Apple's page of subscriptions.

Delete all statistics

In the same danger zone, Delete all statistics wipes the games played, the points, the best scores and the days each star chain is counted from, for every player on the device, and in the account if one is signed in. Names, avatars, worlds and settings, the weekly goal of stars among them, stay as they are.

Delete one player

Open that player in SYVT and choose Delete this player, then confirm. Their nickname, avatar, scores, days played and settings leave the device immediately, and if you are signed in they are removed from your account and from every other device signed in to it.

If you have never signed in, this is the only deletion there is to make: without an account, nothing about your players ever left the device. If you bought SYVT+ in the App Store, our server has a record of the purchase, as the Privacy Policy describes, but nothing in it says whose it is.

Delete everything on a device

Uninstalling SYVT removes the device's copy of everything — all players, scores and settings, and the recordings of words it has downloaded. On an iPhone or iPad, touch and hold SYVT's icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App, and SYVT installed again starts signed out. Offloading the app instead, under Settings → General → iPhone Storage (iPad Storage on an iPad), keeps the players, the settings and the sign-in for when it comes back. A copy can also stay in the device's own backup, held under your Apple or Google account, until that backup is replaced.

If you cannot open the app: by email

  1. Write to hi@SYVT.me from the email address the account uses.
  2. Use the subject line DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. By default, every account held under your email address for apps published by A51 will be deleted — mention SYVT in the message if you want only this game's data removed.

If the account signs in with Apple and you chose to hide your address, write from any address and give the relay address Apple made for SYVT, the one ending in @privaterelay.appleid.com: the iPhone shows it under Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → SYVT. We confirm by writing to that address, which Apple forwards to you.

What happens next

  • Everything done in the app takes effect immediately. Emailed requests are carried out within 5 business days.
  • Deleting the account removes the sign-in record (the email address, and the stored password hash or the link to the Google or Apple account) and everything the account holds on our servers: the account's document with every player under it, its SYVT+, the records of its purchases and the codes it redeemed.
  • What outlasts it says nothing about whose it was: a code keeps its count of uses; an App Store purchase that Apple refunded keeps the record that it was refunded, so that it cannot be brought into a new account; and an App Store purchase that never joined the account keeps its record, which names nobody.
  • Apple goes on telling our server about an App Store purchase after the account is gone: its next notice — a renewal, a subscription running out, a refund — starts the purchase's record again, without an account.
  • Deletion is permanent: nicknames, avatars, scores and settings cannot be recovered.
  • Residual copies in operational backups are purged within up to 365 days.
