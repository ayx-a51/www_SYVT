Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

SYVT is a spelling game for children. It plays with no connection at all, and an account is optional. This page says exactly what is stored, where it is kept, and how to get rid of it.

Playing without an account

SYVT needs no sign-in. Used that way, everything the game remembers — the players on the device, their nicknames, avatars, chosen world and language, best scores, rounds played, points and settings — is written to the app's own storage on that device and nowhere else. We never see it, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

What an account stores

An account exists so that the same players can be carried to another device, and it is made by a parent in the app's parents' corner. It uses an email address and a password. We receive the email address. The password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which keeps it only as a salted hash, never in readable form, together with the dates the account was created and last used.

Once signed in, the game keeps one document per account on our server. For each player it holds the nickname (up to eight characters, freely chosen), the avatar (a short string of part numbers), the chosen world, the chosen language, the best score in each language, the number of rounds played, the points scored, when the player was made, and the two paid settings — how much arithmetic is mixed in, and which level a round starts at. Alongside those it holds the identifiers of players deleted on another device, the up to two languages a free account has claimed, the time of the last change and a version number.

What we never collect

No real names, no age or date of birth, no address or telephone number, no photographs, no contacts, no location, and no advertising identifier. Nothing a child types is collected beyond the nickname, which need not be a real name. There is no chat and no messaging, and nothing a player does is visible to any other user.

Where it is kept, and how it is protected

Account data sits on Google's Firebase platform, in a project we run. The database (Cloud Firestore) is in Google's European multi-region, with the data held in Belgium and the Netherlands. The one server function the app can call — redeeming a friends-and-family code — runs in Zurich, Switzerland; a second function, which guards the project's spending, runs in Belgium. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and the database rules let an account read and write its own document and nothing else. Google acts as our processor here, on our instructions.

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking

SYVT carries no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking or marketing software. It sets no cookies and reads no advertising identifier. Google Analytics is switched off in the Firebase project. How a child plays never leaves the device, apart from the scores described above, and only while an account is signed in.

What the servers log

As with any app built on Firebase, Google records the IP address and the user agent of the requests the app makes — for sign-in security, and in the log of the function that redeems codes. Those logs are kept briefly and are not used to build a profile. Firebase also gives the installed app a random installation identifier, which Google uses to operate the service; it identifies an installation, not a person, and we do not read it. This website is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, logs the IP address of a visit.

SYVT Pro and purchases

SYVT Pro is sold as a monthly subscription or as a single payment, and is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your payment details — only whether the entitlement is active. The app remembers that answer on the device, so a paid game still works offline.

Friends-and-family codes

Redeeming a code sends the code and your account identifier to our server, which records the code, the account and the moment it was used, so that a code cannot be spent twice, and stores the entitlement it grants — where it came from, the date, and any expiry date — against the account. Nothing else is involved.

Data sharing

We do not sell data, and we share none of it with third parties for their own purposes. The only companies involved are Google — as the operator of Firebase, where account data is stored, and of Google Play, which processes purchases — and GitHub, which serves this website. They act as our processors under contract.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept for as long as the account exists. Deleting a player in the app (Players → Delete this player) removes that player from the device and from the account, on every device signed in to it. Data held only on a device goes when the app is uninstalled. To remove an account and everything under it, follow the Data Deletion Policy.

Children

SYVT is made for children of roughly eight to twelve, and is built so that a child gives nothing away in order to play. No account is needed. The only thing a child enters is a nickname of up to eight characters. There is no advertising, nothing links out of the game to the web, and nothing a child does is shown to anyone else. Purchases and the account live in a parents' corner that opens only after a long press, so that an adult sets them up. If you believe a child has given us more than the above, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the data held under your account, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — see the Data Deletion Policy. You may also simply play without an account, in which case there is nothing on our servers to ask about. Write to hi@SYVT.me for any of these.

Changes to this policy

If we change what SYVT collects, we will update this page and the date at the top of it.
